COMMUNITY MEMBERSHIP

NP Membership Benefits

Created for qualifying non-profit organizations interested in community engagement and networking.

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to selected chamber networking activities.
  • Participation in eligible community events.
  • Access to relevant chamber business information.
  • Opportunities to connect with other organizations.
ENTRY LEVEL MEMBERSHIP

Bronze Membership Benefits

An excellent starting point for businesses seeking exposure and professional connections.

  • Standard business directory listing.
  • Access to selected networking events.
  • Invitations to member business activities.
  • Access to selected business workshops.
  • Opportunities to build professional relationships.
BUSINESS GROWTH MEMBERSHIP

Silver Membership Benefits

Designed for organizations looking to strengthen their market presence and business opportunities.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Enhanced business directory visibility.
  • Access to additional training sessions.
  • Discounts on selected chamber events.
  • Additional promotional opportunities.
  • Priority access to selected networking activities.
PREMIUM MEMBERSHIP

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium membership for organizations seeking greater visibility and business influence.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium business directory placement.
  • Priority business promotion opportunities.
  • Featured business spotlight opportunities.
  • Exclusive invitations to selected chamber events.
  • Enhanced networking and partnership opportunities.
  • Priority access to selected training programmes.
