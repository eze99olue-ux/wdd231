NP Membership Benefits

  • Access to chamber networking opportunities.
  • Participation in selected community activities.
  • Access to relevant business information.
  • Opportunities to connect with other organizations.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Access to chamber networking opportunities.
  • Business directory listing.
  • Access to selected business workshops.
  • Opportunities to connect with other members.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Enhanced business directory visibility.
  • Access to additional business development events.
  • Promotional opportunities through chamber channels.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium business directory visibility.
  • Priority promotional opportunities.
  • Additional networking and partnership opportunities.
  • Enhanced access to selected chamber activities.
